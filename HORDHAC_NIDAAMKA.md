# Hormuud University LMS — Hordhac iyo diyaarinta slides

> Dukumeentigan waxaa loogu talagalay in laga diyaariyo bandhig slides ah. Qoraalku wuxuu sharxayaa dhibaatada, dadka ay saamayso, xalka LMS-ku keenayo, qaybaha nidaamka iyo habka demo-ga. Metrics-ka lagu soo jeediyey waa fikrado la cabbiri karo; ma aha tirooyin natiijo ahaan loo xaqiijiyey.

## 1. Fikradda mashruuca hal jumlad

Hormuud University LMS waa goob waxbarasho oo isku xidha maamulka jaamacadda, macallimiinta iyo ardayda si koorsooyinka, casharrada, assignments-ka, quizzes-ka, natiijooyinka iyo fariimuhu ugu wada jiraan hal nidaam.

## 2. Dhibaatada la beegsanayo

Marka waxbarashadu ku kala baahdo waraaqo, kooxo fariimo, faylal qof walba meel ku kaydsaday iyo habab kala duwan oo dhibcaha loo gaarsiiyo, ardayga iyo macallinkuba way ku adkaan kartaa inay ogaadaan halka casharka ama shaqadu taal iyo waxa xiga ee laga rabo.

### Caqabadaha ay nidaamyada nooca LMS ahi wax ka qaban karaan

- **Casharrada oo kala firdhan:** Ardaygu wuxuu waqti ku lumiyaa raadinta cashar ama faylkii macallinku soo diray.
- **Shaqada iyo deadline-ka oo aan hal meel ka muuqan:** Ardaygu wuxuu ku dhibtoon karaa la socodka assignment-ka, waqtiga kama dambaysta ah iyo xaaladda gudbintiisa.
- **Gudbin iyo sixid aan isku xirnayn:** Faylka ardayga, dhibcaha iyo feedback-ga macallinku waxay ku kala jiri karaan meelo kala duwan.
- **Ardayga oo aan si fudud u arkin natiijadiisa:** Ma jiro bog keliya oo uu ka eego waxa uu gudbiyey iyo grade-ka la siiyey.
- **Maamulka oo aan haysan diiwaan mideysan:** Macallimiinta, ardayda, classes-ka iyo courses-ku waxay u baahan yihiin hab nidaamsan oo loo abuuro loona maamulo.
- **Xiriirka macallinka iyo ardayga oo ku xiran fariimo aan habaysanayn:** Su'aalaha iyo jawaabuhu way ku dhex lumi karaan wada-sheekaysiyo kala duwan.

> **Sida loo soo bandhigo:** Ku sharax kuwan inay yihiin dhibaatooyinka waxbarasho ee LMS-ku u samaysan yahay inuu wax ka qabto. Ha u soo bandhigin inay yihiin cabbir ama natiijo la xaqiijiyey oo jaamacadda laga qaaday haddii aan xog gaar ah la ururin.

## 3. Yaa dhibaatadu saamaysaa?

| Qofka | Waxa ku adkaan kara | Waxa uu LMS-ku siiyo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arday | Helidda koorso, cashar iyo faylal; raacista shaqada; ogaanshaha natiijada | My courses, lesson files, assignments, quizzes, grade/feedback iyo notifications |
| Macallin | Qaybinta casharro iyo assessments; la socodka submissions-ka; gaarsiinta feedback | Teaching workspace, courses loo xilsaaray, lesson management, grading iyo class analytics |
| Maamul | Diiwaangelinta iyo maaraynta qaab-dhismeedka tacliinta iyo users-ka | Faculty, department, class, user iyo course management, reports, settings iyo audit logs |

## 4. Xalka la soo jeediyey

LMS-ku wuxuu bixiyaa hal nidaam oo role-ku xaddidayo waxa qofku arki karo iyo waxa uu qaban karo:

1. Admin-ku wuxuu dhisaa qaab-dhismeedka tacliinta: **Faculty → Department → Class**.
2. Admin-ku wuxuu abuuraa courses, sawirkooda iyo macallinka qaabilsan.
3. Ardaygu wuxuu isdiiwaangeliyaa, wuxuuna doortaa faculty, department iyo class sax ah.
4. Macallinku wuxuu ku daraa lessons, files, assignments iyo quizzes.
5. Ardaygu wuxuu ka shaqeeyaa koorsooyinka class-kiisa, gudbiyaa PDF assignment, wuxuuna ka arkaa natiijadiisa marka la qiimeeyo.
6. Farriimaha, notifications-ka iyo analytics-ku waxay caawiyaan xiriirka iyo la socodka waxbarashada.

## 5. Waxa nidaamku qabanayo

### Maamulka jaamacadda

- Abuurid iyo maarayn faculties, departments iyo classes.
- Abuurid, edit, deactivate iyo tirtirid user records iyadoo digniin la muujiyo marka xog xiriir la leh la tirtirayo.
- Abuurid course, gelin course image iyo u xilsaarid macallin.
- Dejinta sannad-dugsiyeedka, semester-ka, grading scale-ka iyo noocyada faylasha la oggol yahay.
- Analytics, CSV audit-log export iyo system health.

### Shaqada macallinka

- Aragista koorsooyinka iyo classes-ka loo xilsaaray.
- Abuurid iyo daabicid casharro leh qoraal, sawir iyo materials.
- Akhris gudaha bogga ah oo PDF, sawirro, iyo qoraalka laga soo saaro DOCX/PPTX; faylasha sidoo kale waa la soo dejisan karaa.
- Abuurid assignments, attachments iyo deadlines.
- Qiimayn assignments-ka ardayda iyo gelin feedback.
- Abuurid quizzes, maarayn attempt-yada, dhibco u qorid jawaabaha iyo sii-dayn natiijooyin.
- Fariin la wadaagid ardayda ku jirta class-ka macallinka.

### Waayo-aragnimada ardayga

- Isdiiwaangelin iyo helitaan koorsooyinka firfircoon ee class-kiisa.
- Akhris casharro iyo lesson materials gudaha nidaamka ama download.
- Aragista assignments-ka iyo deadline-yada.
- Gudbin PDF assignment; dabadeed aragis faylkii uu diray, grade-ka iyo feedback-ga.
- Ka qaybgal quizzes waqti leh iyo aragista natiijada marka la sii daayo.
- Fariimo u dirid macallimiinta fasalka iyo helid notifications.

## 6. Sidee ayuu u xallinayaa caqabadaha?

| Caqabadda | Qaybta nidaamka | Natiijada la filayo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Casharro iyo files kala firdhan | Courses iyo lesson materials | Ardaygu wuxuu hal meel ka helaa koorsooyinka iyo casharrada class-kiisa. |
| Assignment iyo deadline la illoobo | Assignments iyo notifications | Ardaygu wuxuu arkaa shaqada, waqtiga iyo xaaladda gudbinta. |
| Faylka gudbinta iyo grade-ku kala go'an yihiin | Submission iyo grading | Macallinku wuxuu qiimeeyaa isla submission-ka; ardayguna wuxuu ka arkaa grade/feedback-ga. |
| Quiz lagu qabto hab aan nidaamsanayn | Timed quizzes iyo attempts | Su'aalaha, waqtiga, jawaabaha iyo natiijadu waxay ku jiraan hal workflow. |
| Admin-ku u baahan yahay diiwaan mideysan | Academic/user/course management | Qaab-dhismeedka iyo users-ka ayaa laga maamulaa dashboard-ka admin-ka. |
| Su'aalaha fariimaha ku dhex luma | Private conversations | Macallinka iyo ardaygu waxay yeeshaan wada-sheekaysi qof-qof ah. |
| Maamulku u baahan yahay aragti guud | Analytics iyo reports | Role kasta wuxuu helaa xogta uu xaqa u leeyahay inuu arko. |

## 7. Habka demo-ga bandhigga

Demo-ga gaaban wuxuu muujin karaa sida hal course uu uga shaqeeyo saddexda role:

1. **Admin:** Fur Courses, muuji course iyo sawirkiisa, kadibna muuji macallinka loo xilsaaray.
2. **Teacher:** Gal akoonka macallinka, fur My courses, muuji course-ka, dabadeed cashar ama quiz.
3. **Student:** Gal akoonka ardayga ee class-kaas, muuji course-ka iyo lesson-ka.
4. **File:** Fur lesson material-ka gudaha bogga ama soo deji.
5. **Assignment:** Muuji PDF submission iyo halka teacher-ku grade/feedback geliyo; kadib ardayga tus natiijada.
6. **Communication:** Muuji conversation qof-qof ah ama notification cusub.

> Demo-ga waxaa fiican in lagu isticmaalo xog tijaabo ah; ka qari emails, passwords, student IDs iyo xog qofeed oo dhab ah.

## 8. Qorshe slides ah

### Slide 1 — Cinwaan

**Hormuud University Learning Management System**  
Hoos ku dar: magaca soo-bandhigaha, fasalka/kooxda iyo taariikhda.

### Slide 2 — Xaaladda waxbarashada

Sharax casharrada, assignments-ka, grades-ka iyo fariimaha marka ay ku kala baahsan yihiin habab kala duwan. Isticmaal hal tusaale guud oo arday/macallin ah.

### Slide 3 — Dhibaatada

Soo koob 3–5 caqabadood: helidda casharka, deadline-yada, gudbinta shaqada, grade-ka iyo diiwaanka maamulka.

### Slide 4 — Dadka nidaamku u adeegayo

Saddex kooxood: Admin, Teacher, Student. Mid kasta ku muuji baahidiisa ugu weyn.

### Slide 5 — Xalka

Ku muuji hal meel oo isku xireysa maamulka tacliinta, waxbaridda iyo waxbarashada ardayga. Sawir yar oo muujinaya browser → API → database/uploads ayaa ku habboon.

### Slide 6 — Sida nidaamku u shaqeeyo

Faculty → Department → Class → Course → Lesson/Assignment/Quiz → Submission/Grade. Ku sharax socodka xogta.

### Slide 7 — Awoodaha muhiimka ah

Courses iyo lessons, fayl akhris/download, assignments iyo grading, quizzes, messages/notifications, analytics.

### Slide 8 — Tusaale isticmaal

Sheeko kooban: admin course ayuu sameeyaa, macallin lesson ayuu daabacaa, ardaygu wuu akhriyaa oo assignment buu diraa, macallinku wuu qiimeeyaa, ardayguna grade ayuu arkaa.

Role permissions, login/session, file access la ilaaliyo, audit logs iyo admin management.

### Slide 10 — Qiimaha iyo cabbirka guusha

Sheeg faa'iidada laga filayo, kadib muuji metrics lagu qiimeyn karo marka xog la helo (hoos ka eeg). Ha samayn tirooyin aan la cabbirin.

### Slide 11 — Demo ama screenshots

Ku dar screenshots-ka dashboard-ka admin, My courses ee teacher/student, lesson view iyo assignment result.

### Slide 12 — Gunaanad

Ku soo celi fikradda: hal nidaam oo isku xira maamulka, macallinka iyo ardayga. Ku dhammee waxa xiga ama su'aalaha.

## 9. Sida loo cabbiro guusha

Kuwani waa **metrics la soo jeediyey**, mana aha natiijooyin hadda la xaqiijiyey:

- Tirada ardayda iyo macallimiinta si joogto ah u gala nidaamka.
- Boqolkiiba assignments-ka lagu gudbiyo ka hor deadline-ka.
- Muddada u dhexaysa gudbinta assignment-ka iyo grading-giisa.
- Boqolkiiba lessons-ka la daawaday/la furay marka la daabaco.
- Tirada quizzes la dhammaystiray iyo natiijooyinka la sii daayay.
- Muddada jawaabta fariimaha u dhexaysa macallinka iyo ardayga.
- Jawaab-celinta ardayda/macallimiinta ee sahamin laga qaado.

Si loo soo bandhigo isbeddel, ururi baseline ka hor isticmaalka ama ku dar cabbirro xilli kasta. Ha sheegan in nidaamku kordhiyey dhibcaha ama yareeyey maqnaanshaha haddii aan xog taas caddeynaysa jirin.

## 10. Xadka nidaamka hadda

Qaybaha code-ka hadda muuqda waa maamulka tacliinta, courses, lessons, assignments, quizzes, grade/feedback, messages, notifications, analytics, settings iyo audit. Dukumeentigan ma sheeganayo in nidaamku leeyahay attendance, lacag-bixin, live video classes ama mobile app gaar ah.

Ardaygu wuu isdiiwaangeliyaa oo isla markiiba ayuu galaa; approval workflow uma baahna. Student-ku wuxuu arkaa koorsooyinka firfircoon ee class-kiisa, mana jiro enrollment qof-qof ah oo loo baahan yahay.

## 11. Qoraal tusaale ah oo lagu furo bandhigga

> “Waxbarashada jaamacaddu waxay ku lug leedahay arday, macallin iyo maamul. Marka casharrada, shaqooyinka, natiijooyinka iyo xiriirku kala yaallaan meelo kala duwan, la socodku wuu adkaan karaa. Hormuud University LMS wuxuu qaybahan isugu keenayaa hal goob oo ammaan ah, role kastaana wuxuu helayaa shaqada uu u baahan yahay.”

## 12. Qoraal tusaale ah oo lagu xiro bandhigga

> “Nidaamkani wuxuu ka bilaabmaa maamulka course-ka, wuxuu sii maraa casharka iyo assessment-ka, wuxuuna ku dhammaadaa grade iyo feedback uu ardaygu heli karo. Ujeeddadu waa in waxbarashada iyo xogteedu noqdaan kuwo si fudud loo helo, loo maamulo, loona la socdo.”
